# Team Charter

## 1. Purpose
This team exists to deliver high-quality products and services that create value for our users, stakeholders, and organization. We work collaboratively, communicate clearly, and focus on outcomes that align with the mission of the project and the needs of the people we serve.

## 2. Mission and Objectives
- Build reliable, maintainable, and user-centered solutions.
- Deliver work that is consistent with agreed priorities, scope, and timelines.
- Continuously improve quality, speed, and collaboration through feedback and reflection.
- Support one another in making sound decisions and resolving challenges constructively.

## 3. Core Values
- Respect: We treat each other with professionalism, empathy, and fairness.
- Ownership: We take responsibility for our work, our commitments, and our learning.
- Transparency: We communicate openly, share context, and surface risks early.
- Quality: We care about correctness, usability, maintainability, and performance.
- Collaboration: We work across roles, disciplines, and perspectives to achieve common goals.
- Continuous Improvement: We learn from mistakes, seek feedback, and adapt as needed.

## 4. Working Principles
- Start with clear goals and success criteria.
- Align on responsibilities before work begins.
- Break work into manageable, visible increments.
- Keep documentation current and easy to understand.
- Prefer evidence-based decisions and practical trade-offs.
- Synthesize feedback into action rather than personal conflict.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
### Team Members
- Complete assigned work to agreed quality and timeline standards.
- Communicate blockers, dependencies, and status updates proactively.
- Participate in planning, reviews, and retrospectives.
- Maintain a respectful and constructive working environment.

### Team Lead / Manager
- Set direction, priorities, and guardrails.
- Remove barriers and support team enablement.
- Facilitate alignment across stakeholders and project goals.
- Champion healthy team practices and accountability.

### Stakeholders and Partners
- Provide clear requirements, feedback, and decision support.
- Respect delivery timelines and project constraints.
- Engage in constructive collaboration and timely reviews.

## 6. Communication Expectations
- Use shared channels for project updates, decisions, and important information.
- Provide timely status updates for work in progress, especially when blocked or at risk.
- Document decisions, assumptions, and open questions where relevant.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and respectful.
- Seek clarification early rather than making assumptions that drive rework.

## 7. Decision-Making
- Default to consensus when feasible and timely.
- Escalate unresolved disagreements to the relevant decision-maker or team lead.
- When a decision is required quickly, the accountable owner may decide using the best available information, while documenting rationale and follow-up.
- Decisions should be informed by user value, technical feasibility, risk, and project impact.

## 8. Delivery Standards
- Work should be reviewed before being considered complete.
- Quality checks, testing, and validation should be part of the normal delivery process.
- Changes should be easy to understand, maintain, and support over time.
- Documentation should be updated alongside code or process changes when needed.

## 9. Meeting Cadence
- Use regular planning and review sessions to align priorities and progress.
- Keep meetings focused, actionable, and time-boxed.
- Share concise notes and action items to maintain alignment between sessions.
- Use retrospectives to identify improvements and make them actionable.

## 10. Conflict and Accountability
- Address issues directly, respectfully, and in a timely manner.
- Focus on behaviors, impact, and solutions rather than personal blame.
- Hold one another accountable for commitments without creating fear or defensiveness.
- If a conflict cannot be resolved constructively, escalate through the appropriate channel for support.

## 11. Inclusion and Psychological Safety
- Everyone is expected to contribute ideas, challenge assumptions, and ask questions without fear of ridicule.
- Diverse perspectives are valuable and should be encouraged.
- We aim to create a work environment where people can do their best work and grow professionally.

## 12. Success Measures
We will know this charter is working when:
- The team is aligned on priorities and outcomes.
- Work is delivered predictably with strong quality and ownership.
- Decisions are made transparently and efficiently.
- Team members feel supported, heard, and empowered.
- Continuous improvement becomes a normal part of the team’s practice.

## 13. Review and Renewal
This charter should be reviewed periodically and updated whenever the team’s goals, structure, or operating model materially changes. Review ownership should be shared by the team and leadership so that the charter remains relevant and practical.

## 14. Commitment
By working together, we commit to uphold this charter and to continuously improve how we collaborate, deliver, and support one another.
