## Working agreement
- **Meetings:** at times agreed via WhatsApp; notes recorded in the sprint note.
- **Communication:** WhatsApp for quick questions; important decisions recorded in GitHub Issues.
- **Definition of Done:** merged via PR, reviewed & approved by another member, all tests pass,
  work documented in `/docs`.
- **Code reviews:** every PR reviewed & approved by at least one other member within 24 hours.
- **Commits:** every member makes meaningful commits every week from their own account.
- **Use of AI:** recorded in `AI_USE.md` (tool, why, what we changed). Everyone must be able to
  explain their own contribution at the defence.
- **Conflict & absence:** report problems/absence to the Scrum Lead early; unresolved issues go
  to the project coordinator before the next milestone.
- **Team changes:** none after D1 without written approval.
